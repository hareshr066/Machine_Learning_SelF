India AI Job Market Intelligence :::

 analyzes the actual Indian job
 market for AI, ML and Data Science

 What will this system do?
Imagine you're an AI Engineer looking for placements.

You want answers to questions like:
1. Which skills are companies demanding most?
2. How many Python, SQL, PyTorch and TensorFlow requirements appear in job listings?
3. Which cities have more AI/ML opportunities?
4. What salary ranges are being advertised?
5. Which companies are hiring?
6. How are AI Engineer requirements different from Data Scientist requirements?
We'll collect real job advertisements and turn them into meaningful insights.
